        Saving an RSS or Atom feed makes it the source of the headlines under the clock. Other sources are in Settings › News on the TV.

        Paste your calendar’s private iCal address to see your next event under the clock. Google: Settings › your calendar › Secret address in iCal format. Apple: share the calendar publicly. Outlook: Publish calendar › ICS. Turn it on in Settings › Clock on the TV.
