Shore Companion

Photos and weather for your TV, from your phone.

Paired with your TV

Open companion

To install on iPhone: tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

To install: open your browser menu and choose Install app or Add to Home screen.

Pair with your TV

On the TV, open Shore › Settings › Phone, turn on the companion and scan the code with your phone’s camera.

Your phone talks to the TV directly on your Wi-Fi. The pairing code stays on this phone.
