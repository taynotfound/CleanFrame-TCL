The app collects nothing about you

Shore has no account, no analytics, no advertising ID and no crash reporting. Its settings, PIN, app lists and paired phones are stored on your TV only. I receive no data from the app.

What the app contacts, and why

To show live content, the app connects from your TV directly to the providers below. Each of them necessarily sees your TV's IP address and is bound by its own privacy policy. You can switch these features off in Settings.

  • Weather: Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com); the place you entered is looked up and sent as coordinates.
  • News headlines: the RSS feeds of the publishers you select (for example BBC, DW, Tagesschau, Spiegel, Zeit).
  • Wallpapers: NASA, Wikimedia Commons, Bing, Unsplash, Pexels, Lorem Picsum or Klipy, only if you choose such a source.
  • Synced lyrics: LRCLIB (lrclib.net), receiving the artist and title of the playing song. Lyrics belong to their rights holders.
  • Top Shelf recommendations: ARD, ZDF, ARTE, TMDB, YouTube and Twitch, only with the keys you build in yourself.
  • Phone companion: the companion page is served by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). Your TV's address and the pairing key travel after the # in the link, which browsers never send to the server. Control traffic stays on your local network.

Legal basis for the connections is your choice to use the feature (Art. 6(1)(b) and (a) GDPR, § 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG). I do not store, receive or forward this data.

Parental lock

The PIN and daily limits are saved on the TV only. Shore does not collect data about children and is not aimed at them.
