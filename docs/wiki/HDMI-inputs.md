# HDMI inputs

![Inputs in their own row](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/taynotfound/shore/main/docs/images/tcl/inputs-row.jpg)

Inputs come from Android's TV Input Framework. A device that is **plugged in and switched on** gets a tile; standby or unplugged devices are hidden. OK switches the TV to that input, and inputs you rename in the TV settings keep their name. Otherwise a tile uses the name the device announces over HDMI-CEC, such as *PlayStation 5* or *Apple TV*.

CEC must be on in the TV settings (TCL calls it *T-Link*); without it the port name, such as *HDMI 1*, is shown.

## Where the tiles go

Choose *Settings › Home screen › Inputs › First in Home* or *Own row*. Inputs you never use can be hidden.

## "PlayStation 5 is on"

When a device switches on while Home is showing (or the screensaver runs), Shore asks *PlayStation 5 is on · Switch / Stay*. OK switches to it, and the question goes away after 20 seconds. Turn this off with *Ask* in the same row.

## Names and icons

Each input gets an icon from its name: a controller for *PlayStation* or *Xbox*, a box for *Sky Q*, a disc for a Blu-ray player, a speaker bar for a soundbar, and so on; unknown devices keep the HDMI plug.

To change either, hold OK on the tile (*Rename*, *Icon*) or open *Settings › Inputs* and pick the input. *Name* offers common devices, and the [phone companion](Phone-companion)'s *Apps* tab takes any name you type.
