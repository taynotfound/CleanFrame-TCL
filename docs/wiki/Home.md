# Shore wiki

![Shore's Home screen: the time, the weather, one headline and a dock of apps over a photo of the Golden Gate Bridge](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/taynotfound/shore/main/site/shots/home-news.jpg)

**Shore is a plainer home screen for Android TV and Google TV.** It shows your apps, the devices on HDMI and a photo you chose, with the time, the weather and a headline in the corner. Hold Home and you are back in Google TV.

New here? Start with [Installation](Installation). Something not working? See [Troubleshooting](Troubleshooting).
